Good to know

Frequently asked

Quick answers before you visit.

Do I need a reservation?
Walk-ins are always welcome during opening hours, but booking ahead guarantees your table — especially for groups. Book a table.
Can you cater for allergies or dietary needs?
Yes — let our team know about any allergies or dietary requirements when you book (there’s a notes field) or when you arrive, and we’ll take care of you.
Do you offer takeout?
We do. Call us on 415-111-2233 to order ahead for quick and easy pickup or delivery.
Can you host a group or private gathering?
We’d love to. Send us a message with the topic “Group or private event”, or call us, and we’ll reserve the right space for your group.
When is the last seating?
We seat our last guests one hour before closing — 4pm Monday to Saturday. We’re closed on Sundays.
